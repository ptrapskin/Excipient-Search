Safe Sharps Disposal

Needles, syringes, pen needles, lancets, and auto-injectors.

Never in the trash, recycling, or a medicine drop box.

What counts as sharps

  • Needles and syringes
  • Insulin and other pen needles
  • Finger-stick lancets
  • Auto-injectors with a needle (like EpiPens)

Not sure? Ask your pharmacist.

How to package them

  1. Use a pharmacy sharps container, or a hard plastic bottle with a screw-on lid, like a laundry detergent bottle. Write SHARPS on it. No milk jugs, soda bottles, coffee cans, or bags.
  2. When it is full, close the lid tight. Keep it away from children and pets.
  3. Bring it to a drop-off. Ask which containers they take.
