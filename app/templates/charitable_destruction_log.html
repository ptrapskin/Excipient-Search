Charitable Pharmacy Destruction Log

For medications from charitable donation programs (Dispensary of Hope, Americares, etc.) — not the Wisconsin Drug Repository Program, which has its own Drug Repository Destruction Record tool.

Use this log for medications from charitable donation programs (Dispensary of Hope, Americares, etc.) that are NOT part of the Wisconsin Drug Repository Program — those should use the separate Drug Repository Destruction Record instead. Scan each item's barcode to auto-fill drug name, strength, NDC, lot, and expiration. Enter the quantity and unit (tablets, mL, etc.) for each item, fill in the destruction details once, sign, then generate the printable log. Retention requirements vary by program (e.g. Dispensary of Hope requires records be kept at least 3 years) — check your specific program's requirements.
