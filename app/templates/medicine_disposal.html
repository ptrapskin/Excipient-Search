Safe Medication Disposal

How to use a drop box

  1. Keep medicine in its bottle until you reach the box. Follow any posted instructions, since sites can differ.
  2. Many sites ask you to pour pills into a zip bag there. Keep liquids and creams in their original containers.
  3. Cover or cross out your name and prescription information on empty bottles, then recycle them.
  4. Put the sealed bag in the box. Done.

Do not put these in the box:

needles and sharps, EpiPens, IV bags, oxygen tanks, mercury thermometers, aerosol cans, and spray inhalers. Ask your pharmacy or local waste program how to dispose of them. For needles, see Safe Sharps Disposal.

No box nearby?

Ask your pharmacist how to dispose of it safely. Do not flush medicine unless a pharmacist tells you to. If you use the trash: mix pills (don't crush) or liquid with coffee grounds, dirt, or kitty litter, seal it in a bag, and throw it away. Ask first about strong pain medicines (opioids) and pain patches. They can harm others.
