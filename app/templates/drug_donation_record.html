What Can Be Donated

Per Wis. Admin. Code § DHS 148.06, a drug or medical supply is eligible only if it meets all of the following:

  • In its original, unopened, tamper-evident packaging that shows the lot number and expiration date (unopened single-unit-dose packaging is also acceptable)
  • Expiration date is at least 90 days after the donation date
  • Not adulterated or misbranded
  • Accompanied by this signed donation record

Not eligible, regardless of the above:

  • Controlled substances (Wis. Stat. § 961.01(4))
  • Drugs requiring FDA patient-registry enrollment (21 U.S.C. § 355-1(f)(3)(F))
  • Opened, expired, or otherwise adulterated/misbranded items

See the DHS Drug Repository Program consumer guide for full program details.

Recipient Information

Which barcode do I scan?

Most medicine packages have two different barcodes. Only one of them works with this scanner.

✅ Square barcode (2D) — scan this one. It has the lot number and expiration date built in, so scanning it fills those in for you.

❌ Long barcode (linear) — not this one. It usually only identifies the product itself, not the lot number or expiration date, so scanning it won't fill those in.

Tip: look on the box or bottle near the printed lot number and expiration date — the square barcode is usually right next to that text.
