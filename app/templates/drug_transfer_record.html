Which barcode do I scan?

Most medicine packages have two different barcodes. Only one of them works with this scanner.

✅ Square barcode (2D) — scan this one. It has the lot number and expiration date built in, so scanning it fills those in for you.

❌ Long barcode (linear) — not this one. It usually only identifies the product itself, not the lot number or expiration date, so scanning it won't fill those in.

Tip: look on the box or bottle near the printed lot number and expiration date — the square barcode is usually right next to that text.
